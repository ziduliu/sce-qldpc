# TOOLBOX FOR THIS ASSIGNMENT LEVEL

AUTHORISATION. This round you may define a group that appears nowhere in this
prompt, nowhere in the archive and in no registry. Any finite group you can
write down as the interface below is admissible, and the legality contract is
the only thing that decides whether a particular one can be built. Nothing in
this section is a suggestion about which group to choose.

THE GROUP INTERFACE. CUSTOM_GROUP is a code STRING the scaffold evaluates. It
must define exactly two functions:

    valid_q(q) -> bool       the orders your family exists at
    custom_ops(q) -> dict    keys: elements, ident, mul, inv, idx, red

elements lists all q elements, each a tuple of small integers; ident is the
identity; mul(g, h) and inv(g) are the group law; idx(g) is a bijection onto
0..q-1 that the lift uses to index the regular representation; red(g) returns
an element's canonical form. Those elements are what SLOT 3 draws from through
G (G.elements, G.e, G.mul, G.inv, G.pick), so changing the group changes what
every cell can contain.

THE LEGALITY CONTRACT. Before anything is built, the scaffold checks the
identity, the inverses and associativity of your ops and rejects a group that
fails any of them. n = (ca*cb + ra*rb) * q must land in the window stated in
the SCORING section for at least one q with valid_q(q) true, or the program is
rejected before it is built; the largest such q is the one used. An HX row
covers ca + rb cells and an HZ row ra + cb cells, counted in group elements,
and both totals are gated against the cap in the SCORING section. A new family
usually has different reachable orders, so the shape may have to move with the
group - that is allowed this round.
